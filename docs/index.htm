How can we help?

Practical senior support for game teams when it matters most.

Fractional Technical Leadership & Team Development

Need experienced technical leadership to help your team grow?

Get senior technical support to shape a practical plan, make confident decisions, and keep work focused on what matters most for your game. As your team grows, we can help hire and support engineers, establish sensible ways of working, and build systems that help everyone do better work. When needed, we can get hands-on with the code too.

Hands-on Engineering, Prototyping & R&D

Need experienced help turning an idea into a working, shippable part of your game?

Bring in senior engineering support for critical systems, features, prototypes, and technical experiments. We can take ownership of complex implementation and turn uncertainty into useful, working progress.

Performance Optimisation & Technical Rescue

Is your game struggling to hit performance targets, or has a technical problem started blocking progress?

When a game is struggling to perform or a project has become difficult to move forward, we find the technical and delivery issues holding it back. Then we prioritise practical fixes that stabilise the project, unblock the team, and get progress moving again.

Technical Audits & Due Diligence

Need an independent view of a game's technology before making an important decision?

We review code, architecture, tools, and delivery setup to identify strengths, risks, and priorities - giving you a clear technical picture before investment, a publishing deal, a milestone, or the next phase of a game.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us a little about your project and we'll work out the right next step together.

About Garden Shed Games

Built for small teams and indies.

Garden Shed Games was created to help game-makers navigate the technical complexity of making games.

Across a 15-year career, I have spent most of my time working in startups and small companies. I know the pace, trade-offs, and shared responsibility of a lean team, where everyone wears several hats and each person can have a great impact on the work.

That is the environment I enjoy most. I like moving between the bigger technical picture and the details that unblock a team - whether that means helping shape a plan, working through a difficult problem, or getting hands-on with implementation. The creativity and enthusiasm of people making new games is a big part of why I choose to work in this space.

Have a game in the works? I'd love to hear about it.
